# ScholarBridge AI

## AI-Assisted Scholarship and Fellowship Management System

**Find. Apply. Track. Succeed.**

ScholarBridge AI is a prototype application developed to make the scholarship and fellowship process easier for Scheduled Tribe students.

The project brings scholarship discovery, eligibility information, application support, document management and application tracking into one platform.

## About the Project

Students often have difficulty finding the right scholarship, understanding eligibility conditions, knowing which documents are required and tracking their application after submission.

ScholarBridge AI is designed to address these issues through a simple digital workflow with AI-assisted features.

## Main Features

### Scholarship Discovery

Students can provide their basic academic and personal information to find scholarships that may be relevant to them.

### Eligibility Guidance

The application provides information about eligibility conditions so students can understand the requirements before starting an application.

### Application Support

Students can go through the scholarship details and start the application from the platform.

### Document Management

The Documents section helps students view the documents required for an application and identify items that may still need attention.

### AI-Assisted Verification

The system can perform preliminary checks on application information and documents and flag information that may require further review.

AI only assists with the verification process. The final decision remains with the authorized officer and official process.

### Application Tracking

Students receive an application reference number and can follow the progress of their application.

Example workflow:

`Application Submitted → Documents Received → AI-Assisted Verification → Officer Review → Official Decision`

### Voice Assistance

ScholarBridge AI also includes a voice-based assistance concept for providing basic scholarship guidance, especially when internet access is limited.

### Multilingual Support

The application is designed with support for multiple Indian languages to make scholarship information easier to understand.

### Officer Dashboard

Authorized officers can view application information and AI-assisted verification results and identify applications that may require attention.
